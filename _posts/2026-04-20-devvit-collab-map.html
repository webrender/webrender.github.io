devvit-collab-map

A Reddit app, built on Devvit, that reads a subreddit's posts and comments and plots every place mentioned in them onto a shared interactive map.

Extraction runs through Google Gemini, which pulls out place names along with a type - hotel, activity, food, shopping - and a sentiment for each mention. Markers are coloured by that sentiment, so a subreddit's collective opinion of somewhere shows up at a glance, and clicking a marker lists the individual mentions with links back to the source threads. Moderators supply their own Gemini key in the app's subreddit settings and get tools to prune the place data.

TypeScript, Devvit, Google Gemini
