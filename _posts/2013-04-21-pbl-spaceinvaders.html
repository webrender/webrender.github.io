pbl_spaceinvaders

A Space Invaders watchface for the original Pebble smartwatch. The invaders march across the e-paper display to tell the time, rendered in the 1-bit style the hardware was built for.

This was written against the Pebble SDK 1.0, back when watchfaces were plain C compiled against a very small set of graphics primitives - no animation framework, no app store, just a framebuffer and a tick handler.

C, Pebble SDK

Source available on Github.
